## What was built

### Summary

The ochre taxonomy band is now on every taxon page in the same place: in the page's full-width bleed straight under the masthead (no negative margins), its path in the masthead's frame, one line at a fixed 48px (42px on phones). Taxonomy pages at every level, species, named and formula hybrids, varieties and the source comparison all build it from data through one component, so the crumbs read the same everywhere; the old 'Taxonomy: Quercus (genus) ›' breadcrumb is gone. A hybrid with no ranks of its own sits where both its parents do, a variety sits under its species. The species and taxonomy title rows moved into the page head like every other page. On phones a short path starts at the gutter and a long one scrolls, pinned to the current taxon, fading at its left edge only while steps are scrolled off.

### UI tour

1. Open localhost:4460/taxonomy/Quercus/Lobatae, then /species/shumardii: the ochre band sits right under the masthead at the same height on both, reading Quercus › subg. Quercus › sect. Lobatae (› subsect. Coccineae › Q. shumardii complex › Q. shumardii), the current taxon heavier; the title row follows under it.
2. Open /species/alba/compare: the same band, ending on Q. alba, above Back to species and the title.
3. Open /species/× alentejana (a named hybrid with no ranks stored): the band reads Quercus › subg. Quercus › sect. Quercus › Q. × alentejana, the ranks both parents share. Open /species/× albescens: Quercus › Q. × albescens (its parents are in different subgenera).
4. Open /species/agrifolia × parvula: Quercus › subg. Quercus › sect. Lobatae › subsect. Agrifoliae › Q. agrifolia × parvula.
5. Open /species/durata gabrielensis: the band ends Q. berberidifolia complex › Q. durata › Q. durata gabrielensis, Q. durata linking to its species page.
6. Narrow the window to about 800px on /species/shumardii: the band stays one line at the same height; its left end fades and a thin ink scrollbar runs along its foot; scroll it back to the start and the fade goes.
7. At phone width (about 390px): /taxonomy/Quercus/Lobatae's band starts at the left gutter, in line with the title, no fade; /species/shumardii's band is scrolled to Q. shumardii at the right, fading at the left; the band is 42px tall on every page.
8. Open /dev/components#band: the band for a section, a species, a named hybrid, a formula hybrid, parents in two subgenera and a variety.

## How it was built

### Design notes

Code-led polish through Impeccable (context, the taxon page surface brief, craft floor, the detector on every changed UI file with no non-advisory findings, and a fresh finish reviewer looking at captures of every taxon page type together at 1920, 1440, 1024, 800 and 390, logged in and out). No comp, so build-phase was not used.

Placement. The band goes in <.page>'s existing :bleed slot (decision 851f), first, on every taxon page. The bleed already renders full width straight under the masthead with no negative margins, so a new slot in app.html.heex (idea 84c2) would only have made every page hand the band up to the layout for the same result. The band's path keeps the masthead's 1400px frame rather than the page's column, which is what makes it stand at the same place on column pages (species, taxonomy) and the frame page (compare); measured with Playwright, its top, height and first crumb's left edge are identical on all eleven pages at each width. The .taxonomy-page-band negative margins and 100vmax border-image, and the V1 .taxonomy-nav breadcrumb and its CSS, are deleted (app.css 3050 to 2979 lines, budget lowered).

One crumb format, by code. taxonomy_band now takes data, not markup: ranks (linked steps, outermost first) and current (a step), each a map with level, name and hybrid; a private band_step sets the rank word (subg., sect., subsect. before, complex after) and the sci_name by level (genus to subsection bare, complex Q.) or, for a species step, abbreviated with its hybrid sign. Its :rank slot and :current's rank/rank_after (decision 24dd) are gone, so no page can set a crumb its own way. The steps come from OaksWeb.TaxonPath (ranks/1 adds the genus and each step's path, skipping missing ranks as the taxonomy pages' own URLs do; current/1 for a species record), fed by the taxonomy page's resolved taxa or by the new Oaks.Species.placement/1. The home page's oak at random and the gallery use the same path, so home lost its private rank list and taxonomy_live its rank_word, build_breadcrumbs and taxonomy_url.

Where a species sits (Oaks.Species.placement/1, domain, since it reads other records): its stored subgenus, section, subsection and complex; a hybrid with none stored sits where both parents do, from the subgenus down to the last rank they share (the genus alone when they part at the subgenus); a variety or subspecies (a non-formula name of more than one word whose species exists) gets its species as a linked step. I checked the data before choosing: every formula hybrid's stored ranks already equal its parents' shared ranks, 62 named hybrids have none stored (they showed no breadcrumb at all before) and gain a path, and 9 named hybrids have stored ranks that disagree with their parents (filed as bug a946); stored ranks win because they are what the taxonomy pages list the hybrid under and what Jeff edits. Computing every hybrid from its parents was rejected for that reason. All 68 varieties' species exist.

Fixed height. The path is one nowrap line at every width, 48px (42px under 760px), scrolling only when too long; the longest path on the site (a variety under a complex) fits one line at 1024px. The scroller runs out over the gutter and is padded back so names keep the frame's edge and a focus ring isn't clipped. After the finish review: a path that fits starts at the gutter with no fade (on phones it used to be inset to 28px with its first letters faded); the left fade shows only while steps are scrolled off (data-scrolled from the hook's scroll listener); a mouse gets a thin ink scrollbar on an overflowing path (scrollbar-width thin, so it needs Chrome 121+ or Safari 18.2+; headless Chromium hides scrollbars, so I verified the computed style rather than a capture); and PinCurrentTaxon re-measures when the path's last step resizes (a ResizeObserver), so a cold load measured in fallback fonts corrects itself when the band's fonts arrive.

Title rows. With the band in the bleed, the species and taxonomy title rows move into the page :head like every other page (bf3f said this waited for this item); the compare page keeps its back link and title in the head under the band, with the species as the band's current taxon, unlinked, as on the species page.

Out of scope: the band's siblings menu stays in the component, unused by any page (idea 84c2 notes it). Docs: DESIGN.md (Layout, the band paragraph, the band token), CODING_STANDARDS.md (page, title row and breadcrumb rows), the component and hook docs, and a gallery section showing the band for a section, a species, a named hybrid, a formula hybrid, parents in two subgenera and a variety. Tests: placement/1, TaxonPath (with doctests), the band component, and the band and head placement on species, hybrid, variety, compare and taxonomy pages.
