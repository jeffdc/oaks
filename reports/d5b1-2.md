### Design notes

Code-led, through Impeccable (context, craft floor, the detector on every changed file, and a fresh finish reviewer over captures of fifteen pages at 1920, 1440 and 390). No comp, so build-phase was not used. I had no way to ask Jeff structured questions from this session, so the width value and page assignments were judged from the brief and recorded as decision 5d56.

The change is in the shared component only, plus one attribute on four pages. `<.page>` gained a `column` width and it became the default, so every page that took the default frame (species list and page, taxonomy, search, sources, source page, articles list, analytics) moved into the column without touching its markup; that kept the diff off the LiveViews item a6c7 is rewriting. The four pages that keep the frame (home, compare, merge, the dev gallery) got `width="frame"` on their `<.page>` line and nothing else.

CSS: `.oc-pg-body` now caps at `calc(var(--container-column) + 2 * var(--spacing-s6))`, centred, and `.oc-pg-frame` lifts the cap back to `--container-frame`. Read and form keep their existing measure rules on `.oc-pg-measure`, so they inherit the column for free: the head spans 960, the content holds 612 or 640 from the column's left edge. Why 960px of content (60rem): it is Jeff's figure, and it fits the type. Rejected: a separate head width for read and form pages (the reviewer and decision a0ac both want one title rule per column), and touching the error pages (Jeff said leave them; 8f1d owns them).
