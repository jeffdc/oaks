## How it was built

### Design notes

The fix is in the library, then every page moves onto it. CoreComponents.header gains a :meta slot (one entry per fact, each rendered as an item in one wrapping line) and :rest for an id; its markup is a .oc-title-text column (h1, then .oc-title-meta, then .oc-title-sub) beside .oc-title-actions. The CSS in components.css is the whole contract: .oc-title is a wrapping flex row with align-items: baseline, the text column is flex 1 1 20rem with min-width 0, so the actions sit on the title's first baseline and, when the column can't keep 20rem beside them, the flex wrap drops them below the text at the left edge (justify-content space-between puts a lone item at the start). No media or container query is needed and no divider can be orphaned. The meta line is Archivo 600 14px ink-2 with lining figures, 16px gaps and no separators, with a strong child for a total. A back link in the page's :head gets 16px above the title from one rule (.oc-pg-head > .oc-back + .oc-title).

Alignment: the item allowed the h1's last baseline or a single chosen rule. I chose the first baseline: in a flex row it is the text column's own baseline, so it needs no restructuring of the markup, it keeps the buttons level with the name they act on however long the meta and subtitle run, and on a two-line article title the buttons sit level with the first line. A grid with last-baseline alignment and a container query was considered and rejected: it needs a breakpoint that can't know how wide the actions are, and last baseline in grid isn't safe in older Safari.

Pages: species (its own .species-header with the hairline divider and Delete offset goes; kind word and IUCN stamp are meta; the V1 breadcrumb stays above as its own element), taxonomy (Subgenus and the species count become meta, the middot goes), source detail (into :head with its back link; type stamp is meta; the page-made .action-btn links become compact <.button>s; the title uses <.work_title>), articles list and sources list (the [&>div:first-child]:flex-[1_1_20rem] overrides and margin overrides go; into :head), article (the hand-built Tailwind row becomes the header: byline, date, Draft and tags as meta), species list (counts_bar helper and .species-counts CSS replaced by three meta entries), search (the empty page's .search-title h1), compare (.comparison-header/.comparison-title), merge (into :head), the article and source forms (their own smaller h1s), species, taxon and species-source forms (wrapper divs and .species-form-title go; the species name moves from subtitle to meta). Species and taxonomy keep the row in the body because the breadcrumb/band sits above it and item 0a43 will move the band; not-found branches are untouched for item 8f1d. Search results keep their counts bar and sr-only h1, asked as efc6 since a visible results title is bug 5148.

Deleted CSS: .species-header family, .source-header family, the dead .action-btn/.edit-content-btn/.create-taxon-btn block, .comparison-header/.comparison-title, .search-title, .species-counts, .taxon-title rules, .species-form-title; app.css budget 3288 to 3054. Bug 17bc: .prose-content h5 and h6 share h4's rule.

Docs: DESIGN.md gets a Title Row component section and the Display line now covers every page title; CODING_STANDARDS.md's Components row describes the contract; the gallery has a Title row section with a record, a list, a work title and two rows at phone width.
