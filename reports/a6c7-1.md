## What was built

### Summary

Every scientific name on the site is now set by one component, <.sci_name>, by its role: display in title rows, headline in comparison column heads, title (18px) for names that are list entries, meta (14px) in metadata lines, and the surrounding size in prose, dialogs and fields; the band keeps its 16px voice with the current taxon heavier. The genus follows one rule (Quercus in titles, at a first mention and in stand-alone lists; Q. in lists under a named taxon or record, later mentions, column heads and complexes; none above species, after with, and for synonyms). The component gained size, level and stored-authority splitting and the band's current taxon a rank word; every page helper and the app.css name rules are gone (app.css 3493 to 3288 lines), check-ui passes with no sci-name allowances, and the rules are in CODING_STANDARDS.md, DESIGN.md and the gallery.

### UI tour

4. Open http://localhost:4460/species/agrifolia × parvula: the title is Quercus agrifolia × parvula, and at phone width it breaks only after Quercus, never at the ×.
