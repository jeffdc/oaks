## What was built

### Summary

Every page is on <.page> and starts at the same left edge as the masthead's acorn (x=52 at 1440, 32 at 1024, 16 on phones). One rule picks the width: the 1400px frame for lists, tables, taxon pages, comparisons, search, the source page and analytics; a 70ch reading measure (612px) for About, Privacy, the Markdown guide and articles; a 640px form measure for every create/edit form and account page. Narrow pages are never centred: <.page> gained a :head slot that runs the full frame, so every title rule spans the same width and only the content under it is narrower. The per-page max-w/mx-auto wrappers and .page-read, .page-wide, .settings-container, .source-detail and the search results' 56rem cap are deleted; check-ui's width allowances are gone except error_html's (item 8f1d) and the app.css budget fell from 3532 to 3493. The rule is in CODING_STANDARDS.md's Components table, the component doc and gallery, and DESIGN.md's Layout section (the One Edge Rule).

### UI tour

1. Open http://localhost:4460/species at desktop width: the title and the list start right under the acorn mark, and the title rule runs to the right edge under the search field.
2. Open /about, then /privacy and /help/markdown: the title and its rule are in the same place as on /species; the text under it is a 70ch column starting at the same left edge, not centred.
3. Open /sources, /sources/1, /articles and /search?q=alba: each runs the same full width as /species, from the same left edge (search results no longer stop two-thirds of the way across).
4. Open /users/log-in (logged out): the Log in title and rule span the frame; the form is a 640px column starting at the same left edge.
5. Log in with the dev user. Open /species/alba/edit, /taxonomy/1/edit, /sources/1/edit, /articles/new and /settings: the back link and title start at the same left edge as every other page; the fields are a 640px column under them.
6. Open /analytics and /species/alba/merge/<another species>: they run the full frame from the same edge.
7. Click between any two of these pages: nothing jumps sideways.
8. Narrow the window to about 390px: every page starts 16px from the edge with no sideways scroll.
9. Open /dev/components and find Page: the read and form examples show the title row in the head across the frame and the measure under it.

## How it was built

### Design notes

Code-led, through Impeccable (context, craft floor, the detector on every changed file, and a fresh finish reviewer looking at captures of every page together at 1440 and 390). No comp, so build-phase was not used.

The component came first, as its own commit: `<.page>` (item 1900) already had the three widths and the bleed, but read and form put everything, the title row included, in the narrow measure. I tried both alignments on live pages (About and Log in at 1440, by editing the DOM) before choosing: a title rule that stops at 612 or 640px makes prose and forms read as a left-aligned column floating in an empty frame, the "strange" left justification Jeff objected to on the old home page; a full-frame title rule ending under the masthead's search field ties every page to the same frame, and the measure under it reads as a column of text, as the prototype sets its 70ch columns under a full-frame title row. A centred measure was the other system that could work, but it would move prose and form pages' left edge to about 414px against 52px for lists and taxon pages, the very jump this item removes, so I rejected it rather than mix the two. So `<.page>` gained a `:head` slot that runs the full frame above the measure; its doc, the gallery entry, its test and the CODING_STANDARDS Components row state the width rule and the edge rule.

Then every page went onto `<.page>`: lists, tables, taxon pages, comparisons, search, the source page and analytics on frame; About, Privacy, the Markdown guide and articles on read; every create/edit form and account page (nine controller templates and SettingsLive) on form, with their back link and title row in `:head`. Pages that were already full-frame (species, compare, taxonomy, search) moved onto the component too, so the layout's `<main>` fallback now serves only the error pages (item 8f1d). Deleted: ten max-w/mx-auto wrappers and the page CSS that set widths (.page-read, .page-wide, .settings-container, .source-detail, and the search results' 56rem cap the finish review flagged as a fourth body width). The read pages' section rhythm, which lived on `.page-read > section`, is now `space-y-s7` on the measure, with their title rows in `:head` so it never touches them. Form loading states lost their centring along with the wrappers.

check-ui: every width entry but error_html's is gone from scripts/check-ui.allow and the app.css budget fell from 3532 to 3493. DESIGN.md's Layout section states which width each kind of page takes and the One Edge Rule (the home page alone is centred, as a title page and contents).

Measured with Playwright, logged in and out, on 31 routes: content starts at x=52 at 1440, 32 at 1024 and 16 at 390 on every page but home; widths are 1336/960 (frame), 612 (read) and 640 (form), 358 on phones; no horizontal overflow at 390. Left for triage: lists at the full frame put their meta far from the name (bug 82c0), and the Markdown guide's two columns are tight at 612px (bug e825). The forms' title rows still differ from each other (item 626c).
