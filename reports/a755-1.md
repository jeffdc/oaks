## What was built

### Summary

Synonyms now show their genus through <.sci_name>, abbreviated as names listed under a species (Q. candida Steud., Q. texana Auctores, Q. shumardii var. acerifolia E.J.Palmer 1927), on the species page's Synonyms section and the merge page's Synonyms to add; search shows matched species, not synonyms, so it is unchanged. sci_name gained three things in the library: any capitalised first word is a stated genus and only Quercus is ever abbreviated (so the 34 synonyms in Cyclobalanopsis, Lithocarpus and Pasania read Cyclobalanopsis albicaulis, not Q. Cyclobalanopsis albicaulis); lower-case authorities (auctores, auct, hort, sensu) are split off as the author; and Q. is held to its epithet with a no-break space so no line ends on it. The species page's own synonym/variety splitter is gone (sci_name splits). DESIGN.md, CODING_STANDARDS.md, the sci_name doc and the gallery state the new rule; check-ui passes with no sci-name allowances.

## How it was built

### Design notes

Approach: change the genus setting at the two synonym sites from genus={nil} to abbreviate, since the abbreviation rule (9ac4) puts names listed under a named record at Q. and Jeff's answer to 1159 put synonyms there. A survey of all 1160 stored synonyms in the dev DB showed shapes the component couldn't set right once a genus was added: 34 are stored in another genus (Cyclobalanopsis, Lithocarpus, Pasania), which would have rendered as "Q. Cyclobalanopsis albicaulis", and a few carry lower-case authorities (hypoxantha auctores, repanda hort.), one of which (auctores) passed as an epithet. Both were fixed in the library, not the pages: stated_genus now reads any capitalised first word followed by more words as the genus (a capitalised word alone is still a uninomial), and genus_label abbreviates only Quercus, because C. for Cyclobalanopsis doesn't read on a Quercus page; name_word? excludes a short list of lower-case authority words. I checked that no stored species name or parent starts with a capital, so the wider genus match changes nothing else. The species page had its own regex splitting synonyms and varieties into name and authority; it now passes the stored string whole and lets sci_name split, so there is one splitter. On phones the long synonym runs left Q. orphaned at line ends, so the space after an abbreviated Q. is now no-break everywhere (Quercus in full still breaks after the genus as a6c7 chose for titles). Rejected: a separate synonym component or a synonym attr (the existing genus/abbreviate settings express it); abbreviating other genera. Docs: DESIGN.md's Quercus/Q./none rule, CODING_STANDARDS.md's sci_name row, the component doc and the gallery paragraph move synonyms from "no genus" to Q. Impeccable: context loaded, craft floor read, detector clean on the changed files; the finish review was a short in-thread look at 1440 and 390 screenshots (species synonyms on alba, shumardii, acerifolia, albicaulis, cerris; merge Synonyms to add; the gallery row) rather than the finish-reviewer subagent, given the size of the change.
