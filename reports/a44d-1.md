## What was built

### Summary

The read and form widths of the page component now centre: a read page (About, Privacy, the Markdown guide, an article) is its own centred 720px column and a form or account page a centred 760px column, with the back link, title row, 2px rule and content all at that measure, instead of 612px and 640px from the 960px column's left edge. The change is in the library only (tokens.css, shell.css, the prose block's cap in app.css); no page markup changed. Column and frame pages are unchanged. The page component's doc, the gallery text, a test name, the CODING_STANDARDS.md Components row and DESIGN.md's widths and Centred Column Rule are updated.

## How it was built

### Design notes

Approach: the read and form widths were a max-width on the content (.oc-pg-measure) inside the 960px column, so the head spanned the column and the content hugged its left edge. Now .oc-pg-read and .oc-pg-form set the max-width of the page body itself (measure plus the two 32px gutters), and the body is already margin: 0 auto, so the head and content centre together with no new markup or classes. The page component's HTML is unchanged, so no page needed editing.

Measures: --container-read is 45rem (720px) and --container-form 47.5rem (760px), the ends of Jeff's 720-760 range. Prose takes the lower end to stay readable: at 17px Source Serif it sets about 85 characters a line, the top of comfortable; forms have no measure concern and take 760. The .prose-content block's own 70ch cap (which would have left prose at 612px under a 720px title) now reads var(--container-read), so it fills the read page and caps the Markdown preview in the article form at the same 720px an article shows.

Rejected: keeping the 960px column and centring a measure inside it with margin auto on .oc-pg-measure and the head (two rules doing what the body's own max-width does in one); leaving prose at 70ch under a 720px title (Jeff asked for wider prose, not just a wider title).

Verified with Playwright at 1920, 1440, 1024 and 390 on /about, /privacy, /help/markdown, an article, /users/log-in, /settings, /species/alba/edit, /sources/1/edit and /articles/new: head and content share the left edge and width (read at x=600/360/152/16, 720px; form at x=580/340/132/16, 760px; 358px wide at 390; no horizontal overflow). /species and /sources are unchanged at the 960px column. scripts/check-ui passes (app.css held at its line budget).
