User feedback (BUGS.md, a local gitignored note) from someone who consults on oaks: include info on oak diseases and which species struggle more with them. Red oaks in their area struggle with bacterial leaf scorch, but willow oak has much higher tolerance, similar to the white oaks; Q. lyrata is reportedly highly tolerant of oak wilt. There is a good bit of misinformation about oak diseases, which often leads people to avoid planting oaks.
