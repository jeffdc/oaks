The analytics plug (lib/oaks_web/plugs/analytics.ex) inserts a page_views row for every 404, including unrouted paths. Each row is bounded (PageView validates path <= 2000), but inserts aren't rate-limited or sampled, so a 404 flood (scanners) writes a row per request. Rollup since 623195a keeps the top 50 404s per day in daily_error_stats and prunes raw rows 30 days after their day is rolled up, so the flood rows still sit in the DB for 30 days. The 2026-11-02 volume-full incident showed the 1GB /data volume could fill (now 3GB).

Consider sampling or capping 404 inserts under a flood.
