# Authoring editor

Mode: Operate. Prototype at design/prototype/ed-*.html (static HTML) inside the world in DESIGN.md. Jeff only, behind login, at a desk on a big screen. Three screens in one frame (tabs in the band): Entry, Facts, Conflicts.

## Job

Jeff writes and maintains each taxon's entry in his own words, and reviews what agents propose. Agents never write entry prose; they only gather Jeff's material (verbatim, with sources, duplicates folded) and propose facts with the quote behind each.

## Rules (Jeff, 2026-11-21)

- Opening a taxon loads any pre-existing entry text; with none, the entry is blank. Reviewing the live agent-assembled text is the same screen as writing (pre-existing text arrives flagged for review).
- Entries follow one consistent structure the editor helps keep: first the key sentence (contextual and useful; it is the fragment shown on the taxon page), then the overall account (what it is, what makes it different or special; field-guide prose), then historical context, and more sections to settle later. Readers should know what to expect.
- Default is one taxon at a time; a "this section across taxa" view supports consistency passes.
- How material reaches the draft is a proposal, not decided: an Insert action brings a passage in verbatim with its source; Jeff then edits freely by typing. Inserted text stays traceable until edited.
- Desk, big screen: density and keyboard shortcuts are welcome.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The entry is a structure Jeff fills in his own words: fixed sections with their purpose stated, his material for the focused section at hand, and every inserted passage traceable to where he wrote it. It refuses the blank document and the agent draft.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited from DESIGN.md: ink masthead marked as the editor, the ochre band holding the taxon, the screen tabs (Entry, Facts, Conflicts), save state and Publish; ink on bright white; Archivo frame, Source Serif for the entry text and Jeff's material; sections on 2px rules, never boxed cards; pencil source marks; drawn agree/conflict/open marks.

STORY: Jeff opens a taxon, sees which sections are done, flagged or empty, works the focused section with its material beside it, inserting a passage and rewriting it, checks the key sentence as it will read on the taxon page, then reviews proposed facts and conflicts before publishing.
