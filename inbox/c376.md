Before, the LiveView socket's connect_info had only :session, so every LiveView visitor hashed to sha256(date) and unique visitors were meaningless; the hash also had no secret salt and rows were kept forever, contradicting the privacy page's 'no way to identify'. Now connect_info includes peer_data, user_agent and x_headers; the LiveView IP comes from X-Forwarded-For because fly-client-ip isn't among LiveView's x_headers; the hash uses a random in-memory daily salt; bot filtering is shared by the plug and the hook. Page views roll up hourly into daily_stats, daily_page_stats, daily_referrer_stats and daily_error_stats (top 50 404s per day), and raw rows are pruned 30 days after their day is rolled up. Trade-off: dashboard numbers are unchanged except long-tail 404 counts. Replaces entry c01f.
