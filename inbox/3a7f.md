Before, the slug was regenerated whenever the title changed, so shared links 404'd. Now update_article regenerates it only for articles never published (published_at nil); a published article keeps its slug. Storing old slugs with redirects was the alternative, not taken. Edge cases: accents are stripped by NFD normalization ('Québec' -> 'quebec'), a title with no usable characters gets the base slug 'article', a taken slug gets -2, -3..., and 'new' and 'tags' are reserved (they collide with routes). Title and author are now limited to 200 chars in the base changeset too. Replaces entry 1728, whose detail wrongly said the fallback was article-<id>.
