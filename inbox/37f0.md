Before, merging a synonym into a species dropped the synonym's hybrids, closely_related_to, subspecies_varieties and external_links (leaving one-sided relationships), and hard-coded transferred species_sources to is_preferred: false (so the target could lose its preferred source). Now those lists are unioned into the target, and a transferred source keeps is_preferred when the target has no preferred source. Also decided in the same change: references are rewritten with Oaks.Species.References (excluding source and target); target parents that name either species are cleared automatically, so Save Merge is no longer stuck; shared sources (whose synonym-side text is not copied), same-parent hybrids and list additions are shown as notices in the preview rather than blocking; merging a species into itself and a missing target are refused. Replaces entry 91c6.
